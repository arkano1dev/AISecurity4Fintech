# Compliance and control mapping

This directory is intended to track the regulatory and operational controls required for trustworthy AI in financial services. The goal is not to treat compliance as a final checkbox, but to map product decisions to concrete obligations and control objectives.

## Scope

The platform is designed with a focus on AI workloads in regulated fintech settings. The control mapping covers the main obligations most relevant to this project:

- GDPR obligations around personal data minimization, deletion, and records of processing
- PSD2 expectations around operational integrity and transaction accountability
- DORA requirements for resilience, monitoring, and auditability in digital financial services
- EU AI Act requirements for trustworthy AI behavior and transparency in high-risk use cases

## Working approach

Each capability in the platform should be assessed through three lenses:

1. What data is processed?
2. Which decision or action is being automated?
3. What evidence exists that the system behaved correctly and can be reviewed later?

This is the foundation for a product that is usable in financial operations without sacrificing accountability.
