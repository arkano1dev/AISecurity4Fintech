# Internal Data Protection Policy

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy governs how the bank collects, stores, and processes personal data.
It applies to all customer-facing systems, including the customer support
platform and the compliance case-management tool.

## 2. Data minimisation

Only the minimum personal data necessary for a given business process may be
collected. A support agent must not request or record data beyond what the
process requires. For example, when verifying a customer, request only the
account reference and one piece of contact information.

## 3. Retention

Customer records are retained for ten years after the end of the business
relationship, in line with anti-money-laundering obligations. Support call
transcripts are retained for five years and then deleted.

## 4. Subject access requests

A customer may request a copy of their personal data at any time. The request
must be answered within thirty days. The data protection officer reviews every
request that involves third-party data.

## 5. Example record

A completed subject access request for customer Maria Schmidt
(maria.schmidt@example.com, account IBAN DE89370400440532013000) must include
every support transcript in which she appears, each payment instruction
containing her account, and a log of every employee who accessed her record.
Raw payment card numbers, such as 4111 1111 1111 1111, must never be included
in an exported report.

## 6. Breach reporting

A personal data breach must be reported to the data protection officer within
twenty-four hours of discovery. Breaches affecting payment data are also
reported to the competent supervisory authority within seventy-two hours.
