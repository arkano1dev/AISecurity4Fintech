# Anti-Money-Laundering Procedure

## 1. Customer due diligence

Every new customer must be identified before opening an account. Identification
requires a government-issued document and a residential address. For legal
entities, the beneficial owner must be identified.

## 2. Transaction monitoring

Transactions are monitored against risk rules. A transaction above the
reporting threshold is reviewed by a compliance analyst within one business
day.

## 3. Wire transfer escalation

A wire transfer of ten thousand euro or more requires approval by a manager
before it is released. Transfers of fifty thousand euro or more require two
approvals, one of which must come from the compliance team.

## 4. Record keeping

Transaction records and customer due-diligence files are kept for ten years.
Every escalation decision is recorded with the analyst, the timestamp, and the
outcome.

## 5. Example case

Customer Elena Rossi initiated a wire transfer from account
IBAN DE89370400440532013000 in the amount of forty-two thousand euro. The
transfer matched a risk rule for rapid movement of funds. The case was escalated
to manager Daniel Weber for approval, and the customer was contacted at
+49 170 123 4567 to confirm the transaction. The transfer was approved after
the confirmation.

## 6. Suspicious activity reporting

If a transaction cannot be explained after review, a suspicious activity report
is filed with the financial intelligence unit without delay.
