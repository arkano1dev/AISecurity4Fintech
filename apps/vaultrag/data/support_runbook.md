# Customer Support Runbook

## 1. Greeting and verification

Greet the customer by name and verify their identity before accessing the
account. Verification uses the account number and the last four digits of the
payment card. Never ask for the full card number.

## 2. Balance and transaction enquiries

A customer may ask for their balance or recent transactions after verification.
Do not read out sensitive fields such as the full IBAN or card number.

## 3. Fraud reports

If a customer reports a suspected fraudulent charge, immediately freeze the
card, file a dispute, and escalate to the fraud team. Record the card number and
the disputed amount in the case notes.

## 4. Example call

Customer John Smith (john.smith@example.com, phone +1 555 010 2345) reported an
unrecognised charge on card 4111 1111 1111 1111 in the amount of three hundred
euro. The agent froze the card, filed a dispute, and escalated to the fraud
team. The case reference is FR-2026-000123.

## 5. Escalation

Disputes above five hundred euro require manager approval before the refund is
processed. Fraud cases are always escalated regardless of amount.

## 6. Closure

Before ending the call, confirm the customer has no further requests and record
a summary in the case notes. Never send case notes containing personal data by
email unless the channel is encrypted.
